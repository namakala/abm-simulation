Resilience Activation — Coping Outcome Dynamics

Overview

This demo varies the challenge and hindrance inputs across [0, 1] and three neighbor-affect levels to show how coping probability, resilience effects, and resource costs respond to the challenge/hindrance balance.

The theory predicts:

  • Coping probability increases with challenge/hindrance ratio. Higher challenge relative to hindrance → higher coping chance.
  • Neighbor affect modulation: negative neighbor affects lower coping probability; positive neighbor affects raise it.
  • Delta stress and affect: successful coping reduces stress and boosts affect; failure does the opposite.

Observed Patterns

  • Coping probability rises with the challenge/hindrance ratio. Pure challenge (no hindrance) gives near-certain coping; pure hindrance (no challenge) gives near-zero coping.
  • Neighbor affect shifts the curve: supportive networks (positive affect) improve coping odds; toxic networks (negative affect) reduce them.
  • Delta stress and affect: successful coping (green) reduces stress and improves affect; failed coping (red) does the reverse.
  • Resilience effect: proportional to the challenge/hindrance gap, moderated by success.
